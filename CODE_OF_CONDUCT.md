# Code of Conduct

AgentStudio follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be kind, and assume good intent. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't accepted, in any of the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project).

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, report it privately to the maintainers: open a private security advisory (**Security → Report a vulnerability**) and say it's a conduct report, or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide on the response, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: from a private correction to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.
